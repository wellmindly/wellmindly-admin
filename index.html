Active Standard v2026.4

Complete operational and clinical manual governing WellMindly. All counselors, university administrators, moderators, and support engineers are bound by these protocols.

1. Student Data Privacy & FERPA / HIPAA Compliance Standard

Section 101 • Institutional Security & Student Rights
Mandatory Compliance

Core Principle: All student psychological screening scores, clinical check-ins, journal entries, and counselor appointment histories constitute strictly confidential educational and wellness records under FERPA regulations and HIPAA privacy frameworks.

  • Encryption Standard: All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in-flight over TLS 1.3. Raw database connections require SSL certificate verification.
  • Anonymity in Peer Spaces: In TalkMindly peer spaces, student real names and institutional IDs are strictly decoupled. System-generated pseudonyms protect user privacy.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Counselors may only view records for students with active or scheduled appointments. General campus administrators cannot view clinical assessment answer sheets.
  • Right to Erasure & Export: Students hold the right to export longitudinal records or submit an account anonymization request via support.

2. Clinical Emergency & Crisis Escalation Protocol

Section 201 • Life Safety & High Distress Triage
Life Safety Protocol

Trigger Criteria: Any student assessment scoring PHQ-9 Question 9 ≥ 1 (thoughts of self-harm or suicide), or any TalkMindly message containing suicidal intent, triggers immediate systemic crisis escalation.

  • Immediate Client Interruption: The student UI immediately renders the Emergency Crisis Modal with 1-click links to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call/text 988), Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741), and campus security dispatch.
  • Platform Triage Banner: Super Admin dashboard receives a high-priority alert with immediate red glowing badge in the Requires Attention feed.
  • Counselor Priority Routing: If the student has an assigned counselor, the counselor is notified via urgent portal alert and email to initiate welfare contact.
  • Non-Delayed Response SLA: Clinical review of critical alerts must commence within 15 minutes during operating hours.

3. Counselor Code of Ethics & Professional Practice

Section 301 • Clinical Credentialing & Ethical Conduct
Licensed Standards

Scope & Qualification: All practicing WellMindly therapists must hold valid state licensure (e.g. LCSW, LMFT, LPC, PsyD, PhD, MD) or verified professional mental health coaching certification.

  • Mandatory Documentation: Counselors must submit objective, confidential post-session evaluations in the Counselor Portal within 24 hours of session completion.
  • Dual Relationship Prohibition: Counselors are prohibited from engaging in personal, romantic, or commercial interactions with students outside the platform.
  • Non-Abandonment Rule: Counselors must maintain continuous weekly slot availability as committed during onboarding, and provide at least 30 days advance notice before pausing availability.
  • Dr. Title Regulation: Only counselors holding doctoral credentials (MD, PhD, PsyD) may be designated with the "Dr." prefix in master calendar and directories.

4. Session Booking, 24-Hr Reschedule & No-Show Regulations

Section 401 • Encounter Scheduling & Time Governance
Operational Rule

Scheduling Windows: Sessions are booked in 45-minute clinical windows via Daily.co end-to-end encrypted video rooms.

  • 24-Hour Cancellation Policy: Students or counselors may reschedule or cancel appointments up to 24 hours prior to scheduled start time without restriction.
  • Late Cancellations (<24 hours): Recorded as a Late Cancellation event. Repeated occurrences (>2 per academic semester) trigger an automatic wellness advisor review.
  • No-Show Protocol: Counselors remain in the video waiting room for 15 minutes. If student fails to join, the session is archived as NO_SHOW and an automated welfare outreach email is dispatched.
  • Admin Reassignment Authority: Super Admins possess full authority to reassign appointments in the Master Calendar across active, verified counselors in event of clinical emergencies.

5. TalkMindly Community Civility & Content Moderation Guidelines

Section 501 • Peer Community Safety & Automated Enforcement
Zero-Tolerance

Mission: TalkMindly provides an anonymous, compassionate peer support community for collegiate students navigating academic and emotional stressors.

  • Prohibited Content: Profanity, harassment, bullying, doxxing, academic cheating solicitation, hate speech, and explicit self-harm instructions are strictly blocked.
  • Automated Multi-Layer Defense: Submissions undergo deterministic clinical heuristic scanning followed by Google Gemini AI toxicity analysis before publication. Flagged posts are rejected immediately and routed to the moderation queue.
  • User Flagging Mechanism: Any participant may report a post with a reason (Crisis, Harassment, Inappropriate, Spam). Flagged items are placed into immediate moderator escrow.
  • Moderator Actions: Administrators may Approve (reinstate), Delete (purge), or permanently Mute repeat offenders.

6. Institutional University Access Control & Domain Whitelisting Standards

Section 601 • Campus Eligibility & Counseling Entitlement
Access Governance

Eligibility Rule: To preserve high clinical quality and prevent quota exhaustion, 1-on-1 human counseling sessions are restricted to verified students from partner university institutions or approved bypass accounts.

  • Domain Verification: Students with verified institutional email domains (e.g. @ves.ac.in) receive full counseling booking entitlements automatically upon email confirmation.
  • Bypass Student Accounts: Super Admins can whitelist specific external accounts (e.g. transfer students, athletes, pilot participants) with an explicit recorded business reason.
  • Database Persistence: All access rules are stored in permanent PostgreSQL relational tables (AllowedUniversityDomain and BypassStudentEmail) and persist across system reboots and updates.
  • Real-Time Verification: Email verification tester in the Access Control panel allows administrators to test eligibility immediately before granting access.
